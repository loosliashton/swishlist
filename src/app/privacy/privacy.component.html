Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026

Swishlist ("we", "our", or "the app") is a wishlist and shared list web application available at swishlist.cc. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Account Information: When you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your Google user ID, display name, and email address from Firebase Authentication.
  • List Content: We store the lists and items you create (such as list titles, item names, descriptions, product URLs, and purchased/checked states) as well as your list of owned and saved lists.
  • Local Browser Storage: We store recently viewed list IDs and your per-list spoiler preferences locally in your browser's localStorage.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and associate you with the lists you own and save.
  • To display, update, and share your lists via direct or shortened links.
  • To generate optional item suggestions when you click Suggestions (only list item names, descriptions, and URLs are sent to Google's Gemini API; your personal account information is not included).
  • To format supported Amazon product links with our Amazon Associates affiliate tag (swishlist0c-20) and optional Keepa price history links.

3. Data Storage & Third-Party Services

Swishlist uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Functions) to store and serve application data. Your use of Google Sign-In is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Lists you create can be viewed by anyone who has the list's link or short URL, and viewers on Gift Lists can mark items as purchased.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete any list you own directly from the Swishlist home screen at any time. To request deletion of your user profile or remaining account data, email ashton@swishlist.cc.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Swishlist, please contact ashton@swishlist.cc.
