Wishlists & Shared Lists, Without Spoilers

Swishlist is a free web app for creating, organizing, and sharing gift wishlists and general checklists with friends and family.

Spoiler-Free Gift Lists

Create birthday, holiday, or wedding wishlists and share a short link. Friends and family can mark gifts as purchased so nobody buys duplicates, while keeping purchased status hidden from you.

General Lists

Need a packing list, grocery list, or shared checklist without hidden spoilers? Switch any list to a General List to check off items directly.

Save & Revisit Lists

Sign in with Google to manage your own lists across devices and bookmark lists shared by friends and family in one place.

Smart Extras

Get AI-powered item suggestions based on what is already on your list, and jump straight to Amazon price history tracking on supported links.
